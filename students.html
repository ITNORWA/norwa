Your Gateway to a Career in Water Solutions

At Norwa Africa, we are committed to nurturing the next generation of innovators and leaders. We offer a variety of entry opportunities for students and recent graduates not only students but also individuals with Interest to gain hands-on experience and grow with us.

Explore Our Student Programs

1

Internships & Attachments

Fulfill your university's industrial attachment requirements or voluntarily join us to gain invaluable, practical experience. We offer positions in various departments, from Process Engineering and Quality Management to Finance and Procurement, providing a comprehensive view of a global player in the water industry.

2

Norwa Academy

Become a part of our innovative projects through the Norwa Academy. This program is designed to give you deep insights into the corporate world, allowing you to take on meaningful responsibilities that will enhance your professional and personal development.

3

Graduate Trainee Program

For recent graduates, our trainee program offers a structured pathway into the industry. You will be mentored by our experts and get a chance to contribute to real-world solutions, fast-tracking your career in the water sector.

Who We Are Looking For

Currently enrolled students or recent graduates from recognized institutions.

Proactive and collaborative individuals who can work independently and contribute effectively to a team.

Ambitious learners who are eager to embrace new challenges and are committed to continuous personal and professional development.
