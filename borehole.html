Borehole Rehabilitation

Our technicians handle the installation of all types of borehole pumps, with expertise in submersible pump handling, we offer safe and efficient lifting and lowering services. Routine maintenance is essential for the longevity and perfomance of your borehole. Our maintenance service include thorough diagnostic and flushing to clear any blockages. Regular maintenance checks by our team help identify and resolve issues early, reducing downtime and extending the lifespan of your borehole.

DNA pumps

Our DNA Pumps provide easy access for maintenanace and are versatile for shallow wells, tanks, and reservoirs. They are designed to deliver reliable water flow for various applications, including domestics and agricultural uses.

Submersible pumps

Ideal for deep-well application, our submersible pumps deliver reliable water flow even at significant depths. They are perfect for boreholes and other submerged environments, providing consistent water delivery for drinking, irrigation or industrial purposes.

Solar Pumps
