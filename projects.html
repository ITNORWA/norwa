Rift Valley, Kenya

Fluoride Treatment – Borehole Defluoridation

Segment: Community Water · Challenge: High Fluoride Levels

This plant removes excess fluoride from groundwater to below WHO limits, protecting the community from long-term health effects such as dental and skeletal fluorosis.

  • Optimized adsorbent/ion exchange media for local water chemistry
  • Simple operations with clear backwash procedures
  • Supports central kiosk or piped distribution
