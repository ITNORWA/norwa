Solar-Powered RO System Transforming Water Access in Taveta
Turning Salty Borehole Water into Safe, Reliable Drinking Water

In Taveta, the local borehole water was too saline to use safely. Today, that has changed.

Norwa Africa Ltd has successfully installed a solar-powered reverse osmosis (RO) system that purifies saline borehole water using only energy from the sun. The result is reliable, clean, and affordable water that the community can count on every single day.

This off-grid system is designed to:

  • Treat saline borehole water to safe drinking standards
  • Run entirely on solar power, reducing energy costs
  • Provide a consistent and sustainable water supply
  • Support healthier lives and improved resilience for the community

Step by step, Norwa Africa is rolling out solar-powered water treatment solutions like this across Kenya and beyond — bringing smart, sustainable water technology closer to the people who need it most.

Borehole · Solar Pumping

Solar-Powered Pumping System in Somaliland

From Uncertainty to Reliable Access

Somaliland

From Uncertainty to Reliability — A True Transformation for a Remote Community

project-location: Somaliland · Challenge: Difficult, Unreliable Water Access

For years, this remote community in Somaliland faced a harsh reality: water was difficult, unpredictable, and exhausting to access.

Families walked long distances under extreme heat to fetch water from shallow, unreliable sources. During dry seasons, the struggle intensified — queues grew longer, water quality dropped, and residents often had to choose between unsafe water or no water at all.

The borehole they depended on held great potential, but without electricity and with fuel costs rising, pumping that water became a challenge the community simply could not sustain. Many days, the borehole remained idle. Water shortages became normal. Health risks increased. Livelihoods suffered.

This was the struggle.

The Turning Point: A Solar-Powered Water Pumping System

Norwa Africa stepped in with a solution that transformed the community’s future: a solar-powered water pumping system engineered to provide clean, reliable water every single day — without fuel, without power cuts, and without the heavy financial burden of operating a generator.

This system now:

  • Pumps clean water directly from the borehole using only energy from the sun
  • Eliminates fuel costs, ensuring zero operational energy expenses
  • Operates consistently, even in remote off-grid conditions
  • Reduces time spent fetching water, especially for women and children
  • Improves hygiene, health, and overall quality of life
  • Cuts down the community’s carbon footprint, supporting greener living

What once was uncertain is now dependable. What once was a daily struggle is now a steady, sustainable source of life.

Solar-Powered Water Treatment Plant

Visoi Mahiga Community

Elevated Tank & Community Kiosk

project-location: Visoi Mahiga Community · Focus: 90 m³/day RO system

For years, the residents of Visoi Mahiga Community depended on untreated river water — a source that exposed families to health risks and intensified the impact of fluoride contamination. Today, that reality has changed.

Norwa Africa Ltd is proud to introduce a solar-powered water treatment plant that provides the community with safe, treated, and reliable water, while fully harnessing the power of renewable energy. This innovative system delivers clean water sustainably, reduces long-term operational costs, and minimizes environmental impact.

A Transformative Milestone for the Community

This project goes far beyond the installation of new infrastructure. It stands as a symbol of:

  • Improved public health through access to fluoride-free, treated water
  • Sustainable development powered by clean, renewable solar energy
  • Community empowerment through dependable, long-term water access
  • Effective public–private collaboration focused on real change

It is a clear demonstration of how local innovation can align with global goals — from SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation) to SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy).

A Beacon of Hope for Future Generations

For the people of Visoi, this solar-powered plant represents hope, health, and opportunity. It ensures that children grow up healthier, households thrive, and the community moves toward a more sustainable, resilient future.

As we celebrate this achievement, Norwa Africa Ltd reaffirms its commitment to ensuring clean water access for all and empowering communities across Kenya with innovative, reliable, and environmentally conscious water solutions.

Together, we are building a future where no community is left behind.

Westlands, Nairobi County.

20,000 Litre/Day Water Treatment Plant

Segment: Clean, Safe Drinking Water for 60 Homes

Norwa Africa Ltd is proud to announce the design, supply, and installation of a 20,000 litres per day water treatment plant at Westlands Cottage Apartments in Westlands, Nairobi County. This project marks a significant upgrade in water safety and reliability for the property’s 60 residential units.

Expertly executed by our team — led by Eng. Josphat Njuguna and Technician Bonface Mulanda — the system provides a high-capacity, efficient solution tailored to the development’s water quality needs.

Solving High Fluoride & Water Quality Challenges

The new treatment plant effectively addresses:

  • High fluoride levels
  • Other critical water parameters affecting safety and taste
  • Risks of waterborne diseases
  • Over-dependence on bottled drinking water

Residents now enjoy clean, safe, and reliable drinking water directly at their taps — improving health, reducing expenses, and enhancing overall comfort.
