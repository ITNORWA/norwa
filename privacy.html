Privacy Policy

  1. Home
  2. Privacy
Effective Date: January, 2025

Norwa Africa Ltd ("Norwa," "we," "us," or "our") knows that you care how Personal Information about you is collected, used, shared, and stored. This Privacy Policy explains to an individual ("you," "your," or "their") about their rights regarding their personal data. This policy is applicable to data provided to us directly by the individual and by others.

Policy 1 : Applicable Guideline Show details

In this Policy, "Data Protection Legislation" means all applicable legislation, which relates to the protection of individuals with regards to processing personal data, including the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679. We protect any personal data that you provide us with & ensure it is stored securely. “Personal data” is defined as information relating to an identifiable person who can be directly or indirectly identified in particular by reference to an identifier, which will be referred to for the purposes of this policy. This policy (and any other documents referred to in it) sets out the basis on which any personal data we collect from you, or that you provide to us, will be processed by us when you visit our website: www.norwaafrica.com ("website").

Policy 2 : How We Collect Your Personal Data Show details

We will only gather individual data for bona fide professional purposes. You may provide the data directly to us for predetermined purposes, which mainly involve keeping you updated about our work. We may gather the following data from you, including but not limited to:

  • Your name and gender
  • Postal address
  • Your telephone number and/or fax number
  • Your date of birth
  • Your email address
  • Your location
  • Your legal person or business name
  • Your legal physical address
  • Occupation
  • Data provided by you via our website and/or through social media (such as Facebook, Twitter, etc.), if applicable
  • Any inquiries made through our website or, if applicable, via social media accounts
  • Address information about you from the government or third-party sources for fraud prevention or legal compliance
  • Email communications between you and Norwa Africa Ltd or any other means of communication such as meetings, telephone, mail, etc.
  • Submissions by you to attend events such as conferences, meetings, etc.

Policy 3 : Permissions Show details

We primarily require approval from an individual(s) to collect and store their data in our database. One may choose to withhold certain personal information as they desire, and by doing so, we may not be able to contact you. We ensure that all the data in our database is accurate and updated, and if you desire to make any changes, please feel free to contact us at any time via info@norwaafrica.com.

Policy 4 : How We Use Your Personal Information Show details

We use your Personal Information to operate, maintain, and provide services to you and conduct our business. For example (but not limited to):

  • Responding to your queries or your job application
  • Keeping you up to date on our various activities by sending you newsletters or other promotional communications
  • Conducting research and analyzing data to improve our service or customize our communications with you
  • Communicating with you

Policy 5 : How we share your Information Show details

We will only share your personal information with third parties in ways described in this policy. We may share your personal data as follows, but not limited to:

  • - As required by law, such as to comply with reporting requirements, a subpoena, or similar legal process.
  • - In connection with an investigation of fraud, intellectual property infringement, piracy, or other unlawful activity, or as we believe reasonably necessary to protect or enforce our rights, protect your safety, or the safety of others.
  • - In certain situations, we may be required to disclose personal data in response to lawful requests by public authorities, including to meet national security or law enforcement requirements.
  • - With any other user, contributor, project creator, or third party with your prior consent to do so, as described in this Policy.

Policy 6 : How we store and protect your Information Show details

We may store personal data in locations outside our direct control (for instance, on servers or databases co-located with hosting providers). We follow generally accepted standards to protect the personal information submitted to us, both during transmission and once we receive it. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Policy 7 : Rights Regarding Your Personal Data Show details

You have the entitlement regarding your personal data as indicated in the EU GDPR. We rightfully respect and uphold these rights to the personal data stored. They include:

  • - Right to Access – You have rights to access your personal data, subject to certain conditions.
  • - Right to Correction – You can alter your personal information at any time by contacting us. It is your responsibility to ensure that your personal information is accurate.
  • - Right to Erasure – Your personal data can be erased in cases where the data is no longer required, subject to certain conditions.
  • - Right to Withdraw Consent – You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Your consent will be effective as soon as it is withdrawn and in the future.
  • - Right to Object to or Restrict Processing – You have the right to request us not to process your personal data, subject to certain conditions.
  • - Right to Data Transferability – You have the right to request us to share your personal data in a legible format, subject to certain conditions.

Policy 8 : Compromise of Personal Data Show details

Norwa Africa Ltd has put in place secure systems to safeguard personal data. If your data is compromised, we will address the issue as required. We will promptly notify those persons whose Personal Information is compromised. Notwithstanding, Norwa Africa Ltd does not accept liability for any unintentional disclosure or a disclosure that occurs due to a security breach of our systems or facilities. Please notify us of any breach of personal information at info@norwaafrica.com.

Policy 9 : Changes to This Policy Show details

Our privacy policy may be reviewed and updated regularly, and checking the policies from time to time is recommended. We may notify you of any changes to this policy when necessary.
