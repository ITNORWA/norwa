Whole-House RO Purification System (400L/Day)

This advanced system uses Reverse Osmosis (RO) technology to provide the highest level of purification, removing up to 99% of dissolved contaminants from your water.

Treatment Process:
  1. Pre-filtration: Sediment and carbon filters remove chlorine and particles.
  2. RO Membrane: Separates dissolved salts, heavy metals, and other impurities.
  3. Storage: Purified water is stored in a pressurized tank.
  4. Polishing Filter: A final carbon filter enhances taste.
  5. UV Sterilization: An optional final stage kills any remaining microorganisms.
Benefits:
  • Provides safe, high-quality drinking water on demand.
  • More cost-effective than bottled water.
  • Increases the lifespan of appliances like coffee makers.
